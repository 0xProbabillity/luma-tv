A calm home screen for your TV. Nothing to sell you.

shangOS replaces the launcher on Android TV and Google TV with a fast, quiet one: your apps, what you were watching, and nothing else. No ads, no sponsored rows, no tracking, no account.

DownloadHow to set it up

Private by design

No analytics, no crash reporting, no update check. Weather is off until you turn it on. An offline edition has no internet permission at all.

Made for small sticks

No third-party libraries and a 2.4 MB app. It checks your TV's hardware and picks a light or richer look, and you can override it.

Up Next and Now Playing

Continue watching from your apps, and control what is playing, right on Home. Optional, and each permission is asked for one at a time.

Easy to undo

Give Home back to your TV's own launcher at any time with one command. Nothing is hidden or locked in.

Is it right for you?

shangOS needs Android 12 or newer (API 31). It has no ads, so it has no income: it is a hobby project made in the open, and it is only as good as the testing it gets. Setting it as the home screen takes a computer and a few minutes (guide).

Verify what you install

Each release is signed, ships with a checksum file and a build-provenance attestation, and the signing fingerprint is shown in the app (Settings, Privacy, This build). How to check.
